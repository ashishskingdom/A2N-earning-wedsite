Health Management: Business Ke Saath Sehat Bhi

Ek safal entrepreneur banne ke liye sirf kaam hi nahi, balki Mental aur Physical Health ka dhyan rakhna bhi zaroori hai. Yahan humne entrepreneurs ke liye khaas health tips aur videos shamil kiye hain.

✅ Daily Health Checklist

  • 💧 Hydration: Har ghante paani piyein, dimaag active rehta hai.
  • 👀 20-20-20 Rule: Har 20 min mein, 20 feet door dekhein, 20 second ke liye (aankhon ke liye).
  • 🚶‍♂️ Walk Breaks: Lagataar na baithein, har ghante 5 min walk karein.
  • 🥗 Healthy Snacking: Junk food ki jagah nuts ya fruits khayein.
